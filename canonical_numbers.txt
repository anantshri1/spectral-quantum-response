What this means: 
- FNO significantly better on average
- FNO beats Born on 52% of samples — essentially a coin flip

The FNO's Jacobian is significantly better than first-order perturbation theory in mean (paired t: Δ=0.072, p≈0.001)

Born's catastrophic failures rather than being uniformly more accurate.
